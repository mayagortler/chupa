# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
A broad Israeli audience organizing a private or business event: course or army-unit graduations, bar/bat mitzvahs, weddings, henna nights, brits, birthdays, engagements, end-of-year class events, retirements and company events. Often the person who "got stuck organizing", short on time, deciding on a phone and continuing the conversation in WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
צ׳ופה is Maya Gortler's one-person studio for digital event design: invitations, save-the-dates, videos, slideshows, menus, signs and cards, designed as one matching set. The site lets a visitor pick an event, see what is included and a starting price, answer a short direction questionnaire, and send a ready-made WhatsApp message. Success is a qualified WhatsApp conversation that already contains the event, the items and the direction.

## Positioning
A designer's eye with careful use of technology, without endless back-and-forth: a short questionnaire, a fixed price up front, and focus and thoroughness in the finish. Everything for the event looks like one story, from the invitation to the video.

## Operating Context
Everything is conducted in WhatsApp. Files are delivered digitally (for sending, screening or self-printing). Payment: 50% deposit in Bit, the rest before final delivery; receipts issued (עוסק פטור).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site (HTML, CSS, JS), no backend; deployed on Vercel. The builder runs client-side and ends in a `wa.me` link.
- Hebrew only, right-to-left.
- Prices and per-event content live in `assets/data.js`; several prices are still drafts (see `CONTENT-TODO.md`).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: צ׳ופה, written with a geresh.
- Voice: warm, direct, second-person plural, no jargon, no "digging".
- Visual references chosen by the owner (2026-11-02): printed photo prints and polaroids pinned on a string, paper stationery (envelopes, postcards, stamps), hand-drawn pencil and gouache illustration, textured paper. Elegant, not childish. No blue in the palette.
- Logo: final files in `brand/logo/` (round badge with a forget-me-not spike, see `brand/logo/README.md`). The site header shows the vertical lockup, centred above the menu (`assets/logo.svg`), and the favicon and apple-touch icon come from the same set.

## Product Principles
1. One story per event: every item is designed as part of a matching set.
2. Short path to WhatsApp: never make the visitor do more than the decision needs.
3. Price is visible early and fixed after the questionnaire.
4. Personal, not corporate: one designer who asks the right questions.
