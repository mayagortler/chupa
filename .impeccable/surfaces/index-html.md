# Home page (index.html)

Scope: home page only; the world rolls out to events, event pages and contact after approval. Visitor mode: Persuade. Action: pick an event and see a price, or open WhatsApp.

## Direction contract

seed: pinned-by-user (owner references, 2026-11-02; no concept roll)

THESIS: The page is a stationery table, because the product is the invitation: printed photo-prints pinned on a string, open envelopes, postcards and a closing kraft card. It refuses the SaaS hero plus icon-card grid and the cartoon party look.

OWN-WORLD: Textured warm paper ground, sage card-stock band, kraft-brown close. Olive ink headings in Frank Ruhl Libre (light), Heebo body. Terracotta as the single accent (envelope liners, stamps, links). Hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache illustrations, wobbly ink line, grainy fill. Wooden pegs, washi tape, deckle shadows. No blue anywhere.

STORY: one designer, one matching set per event; price visible; WhatsApp is the next step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: serif headline and the two actions, then the string of photo-prints (one per event) swaying slowly, each a link.

SIGNATURE: "זה בשבילכם אם" as four open envelopes with the card half out; tapping pulls the card up.

RISK: cream plus serif plus terracotta is a known default look; it is earned only by real objects (prints, envelopes, stamps) and the hand-drawn art, never by color alone.
